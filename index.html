About Golden Sap

Rooted in Tradition, Crafted with Purity

At Golden Sap, we bring you the authentic, unadulterated essence of rural Bengal. Our journey begins in the misty winter mornings of Bengal's date palm groves, where traditional tappers (*siuli*) climb tall trees to harvest fresh, sweet sap (*khejur ras*).

We adhere strictly to time-honored artisanal methods—slow-boiling the sap in open cast-iron pans over wood fires until it transforms into golden Patali blocks or rich, fragrant Nolen Gur. Free from chemical additives, artificial sweeteners, or preservatives.

Heritage & Craftsmanship

The Heritage of Bengal Jaggery

How generations of traditional wisdom shape every single batch we offer.

01

Winter Harvest

Harvesting takes place exclusively during the cooler winter months when the sap runs sweetest and carries its characteristic aroma.

02

Artisanal Boiling

The fresh sap is simmered slowly in wide earthen or iron vessels. Master artisans judge readiness entirely by aroma and texture.

03

Pristine Packaging

Once set into solid Patali molds or bottled as liquid Nolen Gur, our products are securely sealed to lock in farm-fresh flavor.
